# IFMIS & SDMS Modernization — Business Analysis Repository

This repository contains planning materials, templates, and portfolio case studies to support the IFMIS and SDMS technology upgrade program for MINECOFIN. The materials are oriented toward the Business Analyst role and focus on two exemplar projects: Smart School and Excelegde.

Purpose
- Provide a repeatable set of templates and deliverables for business and functional analysis.
- Capture case studies and portfolio evidence demonstrating relevant practical work.
- Produce planning and reporting artifacts aligned with the Terms of Reference (TOR) for the Business Analyst role.

Scope
- Requirements gathering and documentation
- Functional specifications and process mapping
- Validation and stakeholder feedback capture
- Testing inputs and acceptance criteria
- Reporting templates (monthly and end-of-assignment)
- Portfolio case studies (Smart School, Excelegde)

Repository structure
- docs/requirements/ — business requirements templates and examples
- docs/functional/ — functional specification templates
- docs/reports/ — monthly and end-of-assignment report templates
- docs/proposal/ — technical proposal outline and consultant profile
- portfolio/ — case studies and links to code repositories
- templates/ — CV and application letter templates

How to use
1. Start with docs/requirements/business_requirements_TEMPLATE.md to gather stakeholder input.
2. Use docs/functional/functional_spec_TEMPLATE.md to translate requirements into implementation-ready artifacts.
3. Use docs/reports/* templates to report progress monthly and produce the end-of-assignment report.
4. Add links and evidence to portfolio/ to satisfy the evaluation criteria related to shared repositories.

Focus projects
- Smart School — integrated education management platform (example case study in /portfolio)
- Excelegde — automation and analytics toolkit (example case study in /portfolio)
