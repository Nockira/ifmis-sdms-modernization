# Smart School — Case Study

Project: Smart School
Role: Lead Business Analyst / Technical Consultant (example)

1. Problem statement
Governments and schools often struggle with fragmented student records, manual attendance, inconsistent grading processes, and poor parent-teacher communication. Administrative overhead reduces time available for teaching.

2. Scope
- Student information system, attendance, grading, parent portal, teacher workflow dashboard, reporting.

3. Approach
- Requirements elicitation with school administrators, teachers, parents.
- Process mapping of enrollment, attendance, grading, and reporting.
- Functional specification and UI mockups.
- Development oversight and UAT coordination.

4. Technologies (example)
- Frontend: React, Angular
- Backend: Node.js, Java (Spring Boot)
- Database: PostgreSQL
- Hosting: AWS

5. Key deliverables
- Business requirements document
- Functional specification and process maps
- User acceptance test scripts and validation records
- Training materials and administrator guide

6. Outcomes & Impact (example placeholders — replace with metrics)

- Increased accuracy of student records and on-time reporting
- Improved parent engagement through portal usage
