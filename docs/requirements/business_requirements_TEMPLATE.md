# Business Requirements Template

Project: 
Prepared by: 
Date: 

1. Background
- Brief background, context, and drivers for the requirement.

2. Objective
- Clear business objective(s) the change must deliver.

3. Stakeholders
- List of stakeholders (role, organisation, contact) and expected involvement.

4. Scope
- In-scope
- Out-of-scope

5. Current State (As-Is)
- Short description of current process, systems involved, controls, pain points.
- System screenshots, sample forms, and data extracts (attach as annexes).

6. Target State (To-Be)
- High-level description of expected process change and system behaviour.

7. Functional Requirements (numbered)
- FR-001: [Title]
  - Description: 
  - Priority: (Must/Should/Could)
  - Rationale: 
  - Acceptance Criteria: 

8. Non-Functional Requirements
- Performance, scalability, security, availability, maintainability, auditability.

9. Data Requirements
- Key data elements, master data tables, sample payloads, retention and privacy requirements.

10. Integrations
- External systems, APIs, frequency, expected volumes, authentication, transformation rules.

11. Business Rules
- Decision rules, validation logic, and exception handling.

12. Compliance & Controls
- Internal controls, segregation of duties, approvals, audit logs.

13. Reporting & Outputs
- Required reports, dashboards, frequency, recipients, and sample layouts.

14. Acceptance Criteria & User Stories
- User story format: As a <user> I want <capability> so that <benefit>.
- Acceptance criteria in Given/When/Then format.

15. Assumptions & Constraints

16. Risks

17. Traceability
- Mapping of requirements to design components, test cases, and release packages.

Annex A: Stakeholder sign-off
