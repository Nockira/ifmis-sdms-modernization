Budgeting & accounting business processes — guidance and templates

Purpose
Provide templates and structured processes to capture budgeting and accounting flows that can be applied to Smart School and Excledge modules and mapped into IFMIS budgeting and accounting processes.

1. Budgeting process (high-level)
- Step 1: Budget Call and Planning (Entity issues budget templates)
- Step 2: Budget Input & Consolidation (school departments enter proposed budgets)
- Step 3: Review & Approval (finance reviews and approves)
- Step 4: Budget Publication (final budget uploaded into financial system)
- Step 5: Monitoring & Variance Reporting (monthly actual vs budget)

2. Accounting process (high-level)
- Transaction capture (invoice creation, payment capture)
- Journal generation (system posts journal entries mapped to COA)
- Period-end adjustments (accruals, reversals)
- Reconciliation (bank, receivables)
- Financial reporting (monthly, quarterly)

3. Templates included
- Budget template (CSV headers example)
- Journal entry template (date, journal_no, coa_code, debit, credit, description)

4. Mapping to repos
- Smart School: use invoice and fee modules as source for actuals
- Excledge: use sales, subscription, and payments as source for actuals

5. Acceptance criteria
- Budget-to-actual reporting available per period
- Journal entries have traceable source (invoice_id, payment_id)
- Audit trail for period adjustments
