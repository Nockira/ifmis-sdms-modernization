Evidence of direct contribution and repository references — guidance for Nockira

Purpose
Explain how to present the two external repositories as demonstrable evidence of Nockira's direct contribution and prepare artifacts for the TOR submission.

1. What to include as proof of contribution
- Links to commits or PRs where Nockira made changes (if available)
- Screenshots of code or UI with annotations describing your contribution
- Short narrative describing your role and specific tasks (e.g., authored functional spec, mapped COA, led UAT)
- Signed certificate of completion or reference letter from client where you performed the work

- SMT_SCHOOL

  - Backend README: https://github.com/niyobertin/SMT_SCHOOL/tree/dev/smart-school-bn
  - Live demo: https://smt-school.vercel.app

- Excledge
  - Root: https://github.com/TangaHubService/Excledge
  - Backend docs: https://github.com/TangaHubService/Excledge/tree/main/Backend
  - Postman: https://github.com/TangaHubService/Excledge/tree/main/Postman

- "I (Nockira) led the functional analysis for the Fees & Revenue module: I authored the business requirements, defined COA mappings, wrote acceptance criteria, and coordinated UAT with school finance staff. Supporting code and API endpoints are available at <SMT_SCHOOL link> and the backend's Swagger docs."

- A one-page signed statement describing your contribution (PDF)
- Screenshots of PRs/commits showing your username or email
- Copies of any certificate of completion or references
